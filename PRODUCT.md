# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Retail traders tracking their personal forex and crypto trades. They use the journal in a focused, analytical frame of mind — reviewing past performance, recording new trades, and looking for patterns to improve. The tool serves one person at a time (single-user, localStorage).

## Product Purpose

A minimalist trading journal that makes it easy to log trades, track win rates, and review performance over time — without the clutter of professional platforms. Success looks like the trader building a consistent habit of journaling and walking away with clear signals about what's working.

## Positioning

A trading journal that gets out of your way. Minimal, fast, and calm — so you can focus on the data that matters, not the interface.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, Calm, Precise. The tone is quiet and confident — never hypey, never gamified. Every element earns its place. Inspired by the clean, utility-first aesthetic of Linear, Notion, and Arc.

## Anti-references

No childish or gaming aesthetics — no bright neon, no playful illustrations, no gamification badges or rewards. No cluttered dashboards that imitate TradingView or MetaTrader. No dark patterns, no "deal" or "limited time" pressure.

## Design Principles

- **Minimum viable interface** — every element must earn its place. If it doesn't inform a decision or enable an action, remove it.
- **Calm as a feature** — the UI breathes. Generous whitespace, muted tones, intentional motion. The trader should feel settled, not stimulated.
- **Craft over decoration** — brand identity lives in the quality of the execution (spacing, type, alignment, transitions), not in decorative flourishes, gradients, or illustrations.
- **Data first, chrome last** — the numbers drive decisions. Visual hierarchy puts trade data and KPIs above chrome, chrome, and chrome. No frames, shadows, or cards around data that doesn't need them.
- **Progressive depth** — the default view shows what's essential; detail and controls reveal themselves on demand. Never overwhelm at first glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (contrast 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Color is never the sole carrier of information (win/loss uses shape/text as well as color).
- Focus indicators visible on all interactive elements.
